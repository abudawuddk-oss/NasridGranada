## Ikhwan flag
Calligraphy and sword from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Ikhwan.svg (CC BY-SA 3.0). A white hoist band was added, following the historical banner photo https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_ikhwan.jpg (public domain).

## AI-generated images (NOT real likenesses)
The following are AI-generated illustrations, either for fictional characters or for historical people with no known photograph:
Sultan Muhammad XIV, Ibrahim ibn Zamrak, Yahya al-Ansari, Musa Abi al-Ghassan (fictional), Azaiyiz al-Dawish, Meqaid al-Duhainah, Murdhi al-Rafdi, Naif bin Hithlain and Khalid bin Luwai (real people, imagined likenesses), plus the event picture "The Exiles of Sabilla" and the thumbnail background.

**Added in v3.0.0 (all fictional characters, AI-painted portraits):** Qadi Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Rundi, Shaykh Ahmad al-Susi, Umar Farhat al-Qurtubi, Hamza ibn Hassun al-Malaqi, Ilyas Bensaid, Sulayman al-Basri and Abd al-Aziz al-Mutawwa. Each one is also marked as fictional in their in-game description.
The balance-of-power icons for **Tamkin** and **Jihad** are AI-generated emblems. The **Court** and **Brethren** icons are built from the mod's own Nasrid flag and the Ikhwan flag (see above).

**Added in v3.1.0:** 10 AI-generated generic portraits (6 army, 2 navy, 2 political) used for randomly generated Granadan characters. They are not real people.

## Other flags
The Nasrid, Constitutional, Commune and al-Andalus flags are original designs made for this mod. The cosmetic flags *Ikhwan of Najd* and *Emirate of al-Andalus* (v3) reuse the Ikhwan flag.

## 3D unit models (Ikhwan soldiers)
The Ikhwan land units use the **Saudi infantry model (with shemagh) from Kaiserreich**, made by the **Kaiserreich Team** for *Kaiserreich Models - Base Game* (Steam Workshop 2364431052). Files: `gfx/models/units/KR_SAU_infantry.mesh` and `KR_SAU_infantry_diffuse/_normal/_spec.dds`. They are used unchanged under the Kaiserreich Usage Policy (credit given, non-commercial): https://kaiserreich.wiki/Usage_Policy. This mod is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Kaiserreich Team.
The unit setup (`gfx/entities/NSR_ikhwan_units.gfx/.asset`) is based on Road to 56's Algerian infantry entities. Horses, weapons and vehicles are Road to 56 / vanilla assets referenced by name. The smoking idle animation was removed.

## Game art
Focus icons, event pictures and national-spirit pictures not listed here are vanilla Hearts of Iron IV or Road to 56 sprites. They are referenced by name only, and no files are copied.
