# Nasrid Granada: The Ikhwan Exiles
### A Road to 56 submod for Hearts of Iron IV (1.18.*) · version 3.1.1

**What if Granada never fell in 1492?** In 1936 the Nasrid Sultanate still holds Granada and Sevilla at the southern tip of Spain. In 1930, after Ibn Saud crushed the Ikhwan revolt at Sabilla, the British would not hand the rebel tribes (the Mutayr, the Otaibah and the Ajman) back to Riyadh. They shipped them west instead, and the Sultan gave them asylum in the Alpujarras mountains.

Six years later the Brethren have built a **state within the state**, with their own preachers, judges, treasury, religious police and rifles. Muslims from across the world answer their **call to hijrah** (emigration). The Sultan's court is afraid, and Riyadh still demands that Faisal al-Dawish be handed over. **A civil war is coming. You play the Ikhwan.**

Play as the tag **NSR** (the "Nasrid Sultanate of Granada") on the 1936 or 1939 start. It has no bookmark portrait, so pick it from the country list or search for "Granada".

## Installation (step by step)

1. **Subscribe to Road to 56** on the Steam Workshop. Either the stable version or "[Beta]" works.
2. Find your HOI4 **user folder**. This is *not* the Steam install folder:
   - Windows: `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Hearts of Iron IV\mod\`
   - Linux: `~/.local/share/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/`
   - macOS: `~/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/`

   If the `mod` folder doesn't exist, create it.
3. Unzip the download and put **both** of these into that `mod` folder:
   - the folder `NasridGranada`
   - the file `NasridGranada.mod`

   Check that you end up with `...\mod\NasridGranada\descriptor.mod` **and** `...\mod\NasridGranada.mod`. If you see `...\mod\NasridGranada\NasridGranada\...`, the folder is nested one level too deep.
4. Open the **Paradox Launcher**, go to **Playsets**, create a playset, and add **Road to 56** first and **Nasrid Granada** below it.
   **Order matters: this mod must load AFTER Road to 56.**
5. Click **Play**, start a new game and pick the **Nasrid Sultanate of Granada**.

## How to play: a beginner's walkthrough

### Stage 1: The Turmoil (1936 onwards)

At the top of the screen you'll see a **bar with two sides**: **The Court** (left) and **The Brethren** (right). This is a *balance of power* (BoP) bar. Most of your actions push it to the right, and the further right it goes, the stronger your side is when the war comes.

Open the **Decisions** tab (the scroll icon at the top left). Your main menu is **"The State Within the State"**. Its decisions are where most of the game happens:

- **Build the parallel society.** *Found a Hijra*, *Open a Qadi Court*, *Send the Mutawwa*, *Collect the Zakat*, *Arm the Hijra Militia*. Each one raises one of your five **institutions** (see the glossary).
- **Win over the army.** *Preach in the Barracks*, *Win Over the Garrison of…*, *The Sailors of Cádiz*, *The Aviators of Tablada*, plus three court figures you can turn: *Ridwan Bannigash*, *Ali al-Attar* and *Abu al-Qasim al-Sarraj*. Whatever you win over **joins your side** in the civil war.
- **Manage the danger.** Everything you do raises **Fitna**, the court's alarm. When Fitna reaches 100, the Sultan strikes first. *Lie Low* lowers it and buys time; *Sabotage the Vizier* slows the court down.

The **"Call to Hijrah"** menu invites Muslims from the Rif, the French Maghreb, Egypt, India, Najd and the Balkans. They arrive as **muhajirun**, who become manpower and settlers.

The **pre-war focus tree** has 17 focuses, from *The Exiles of Sabilla* to *The Night of the White Turbans*. Completing the last one starts the war.

**The war always comes.** Your choices decide *when* and *how strong each side is*. It begins at whichever of these comes first:
1. you complete the focus **The Night of the White Turbans**, or
2. **Fitna** reaches 100 (the Sultan moves against you), or
3. **1 January 1939** (1 September 1939 on the 1939 start), when the Vizier issues his ultimatum.

The court fights back over time with the *Registration Decree*, the *British Consul's Inquiry* and similar events.

### Stage 2: The civil war

- You keep playing as **NSR**, the Ikhwani side, with **Faisal al-Dawish** as leader.
- **All 11 historical Ikhwan figures** and **every Liwa al-Ikhwan division** are always on your side.
- The **Sultan Muhammad XIV** leads the loyalists with his court, except for anyone you won over.
- States go to the side that has more **Hold** there. The regular army, navy and air force split according to **army loyalty** and the units you won over.
- If you lose, the game is over for the Brethren. If you win, the loyalists are annexed.

### Stage 3: The Ikhwani Emirate (the big tree)

When the war starts, your focus tree is **replaced** by the Ikhwani tree. Use the **search box** at the top of the focus screen to find focuses by name. The tree has these branches, from left to right:

| Branch | Starts with | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **A: The Fitna** (top centre) | *The Morning of the White Turbans* | Win the civil war, then **Proclaim the Ikhwani Emirate**. This renames the country to **Ikhwani Emirate**, raises the **Ikhwan flag** and makes Faisal Amir. |
| **B: The Emirate & the Shura** | *The Majlis of the Sheikhs* | Government, succession, the Shura council, treaties and envoys |
| **C: Tawhid** | *Establish Tawhid* | Religious policy: removing shirk, the sharia courts, the Mutawwa Corps, the Sufi orders |
| **D: Hijrah** | *The Great Hijrah* | Settling muhajirun, new hijras, manpower |
| **E: Economy** | *The Treasury of the Brethren* | Bayt al-Mal, factories, mines, railways, trade |
| **F: Army** | *The Ghazu Tradition* | Cavalry and infantry doctrine, the officer academy, *The Lessons of Sabilla* |
| **G: Navy** | *We Have No Sailors* | Coastal flotillas, the Strait, landing craft, marines, corsairs |
| **H: Air** | *Machines of the Air* | Buying aircraft abroad, a flight school, airfields |
| **I: Diplomacy** | *The Question of Recognition* | Saudi Arabia (reconcile or take revenge), Britain, Italy and Germany, alliances (join the Axis, stay free, or found your own **Dar al-Islam Pact**), the Caliphate question |
| **J: Iberia** | *Jihad Against the Reconquista* | Córdoba, Toledo, Madrid, Aragón, Barcelona, Valencia, the Balearics, Portugal and Gibraltar, ending in **Al-Andalus Restored** |
| **J: Maghreb** | *The Rif Remembers* | The Rif, Fez, Algiers, Constantine and Tunis; arming the tribes; ending in **The Two Shores** |
| **J: East** | *The Sea Road to the East* | Libya and the Sanusi, the Nile, Kuwait, the Hashemites; the **Hejaz and the Holy Cities** |

**The Shura bar (Tamkin ↔ Jihad).** After the war the top bar becomes **the Shura of the Brethren**. It isn't a split between factions: the Brethren are one brotherhood, united in creed. The bar shows what the council has decided to put *first*:
- **Tamkin** (left): *consolidating* the state. Faster construction, research and stability, and cheaper political advisors.
- **Jihad** (right): *the struggle beyond the frontier*. War support, attack and experience, and cheaper army chiefs and high command.

Decisions in **"The Shura of the Brethren"** and many focuses move the bar.

**Other Emirate decision menus:** *Governing the Emirate*, *Removing Shirk*, *The Great Hijrah*, *The People of the Book*, *The Ghazu Beyond the Frontier* (border raids and arming the tribes in the Maghreb) and *The Road to Najd*. The last one lets you fund Ikhwan cells in Saudi Arabia and trigger a **Second Ikhwan Revolt**.

**Expansion is realistic, not free:**
- Each conquest needs the previous region under your control first.
- You can only claim British, French or Italian land when that power is at war, defeated, or no longer a great power.
- Crossing the sea needs the navy focuses.
- Conquered land becomes your core only where your **Hold** is high enough.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in the mod |
|---|---|
| **Ikhwan** ("the Brethren") | The Bedouin religious warrior movement of Najd (1912–1930). In this history they are exiled to al-Andalus in 1930. |
| **Hijra** (settlement) | An Ikhwan farming and military settlement. The first was al-Artawiya in Najd. In the mod they're built in the Alpujarras and beyond. |
| **Hijrah** (emigration) | Leaving one's land for the sake of the faith. The mod's "Call to Hijrah" brings Muslims to al-Andalus. |
| **Muhajirun** | The emigrants who answer the call. They're shown in thousands and become manpower and settlers. |
| **Hold** | The Brethren's grip on a state, from 0 to 100. It decides who gets the state in the civil war, and whether conquered land can become a core. It is shown as the *Ikhwan presence* state modifier. |
| **Fitna** | "Strife." Here it means the court's alarm, from 0 to 100. At 100 the Sultan strikes first. |
| **Army loyalty** | How much of the royal army would stay with the Sultan. Lower is better for you. |
| **Institutions** | The five pillars of the parallel society, each levelled 0–3: hijras, qadi courts, Bayt al-Mal, Mutawwa and hijra militia. |
| **Qadi** | A judge of Islamic law |
| **Bayt al-Mal** | "House of wealth." The Brethren's treasury, funded by zakat. |
| **Zakat** | The obligatory alms tax |
| **Jizya** | The tax paid by non-Muslims (the *People of the Book*: Christians and Jews) under Islamic rule |
| **Mutawwa / Hisba** | Religious police and "commanding right and forbidding wrong" |
| **Tawhid / Shirk** | The oneness of God, and associating partners with God. Removing shirk means closing shrines (qubbas) and Sufi lodges (zawiyas). |
| **Shura** | Consultation. The Emirate's council of sheikhs and scholars, shown as the Tamkin ↔ Jihad bar. |
| **Tamkin** | Consolidating and establishing the state |
| **Ghazu** | The Bedouin raid tradition |
| **Liwa al-Ikhwan** | "Brigade of the Brethren." The Ikhwan division template. Every division built from it counts as Ikhwan and always joins your side. |
| **Harka al-Qabail** | Tribal levies raised in the Maghreb. They are *not* Ikhwan divisions. |
| **BoP** | Balance of power: the two-sided bar at the top of the screen |
| **Focus / decision / national spirit** | Focuses are the tree; decisions are actions you can take; national spirits are the permanent bonuses and penalties shown next to your flag. |

## Testing checklist (optional)

To check that everything works, open the console with the **`** key (or **§** / **^** / **ö** depending on your keyboard) and try the following:

1. A new 1936 game as NSR shows the **17-focus pre-war tree** and the **Court ↔ Brethren** bar.
2. The decisions change Hold, Fitna, muhajirun and army loyalty, and the state modifier shows the Hold tier.
3. `event nsr.300` starts the civil war right away. Check that Faisal leads you and that all the Ikhwan figures are on your side.
4. The **Ikhwani tree** appears. `focus.autocomplete` then clicking focuses completes them instantly for testing.
5. After *Proclaim the Ikhwani Emirate*, the country is named **Ikhwani Emirate** and uses the Ikhwan flag.
6. `observe` lets you watch the AI. `tag NSR` switches back.
7. Check `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Hearts of Iron IV\logs\error.log` for lines mentioning `NSR`.

## Compatibility notes

- **This mod overrides these files:**
  - the R56 state files `history/states/169-Sevilla.txt` and `history/states/173-Granada.txt`;
  - the unit files `history/units/SPR_1936.txt`, `SPR_1936_naval_mtg.txt`, `SPR_1936_air_bba.txt`, `SPR_1936_air_legacy.txt`, `SPA_Civil_War.txt` and `SPR_Civil_War.txt`. These move Spanish units out of Nasrid land.

  If R56 updates these files, re-copy them and re-apply the small NSR changes (search for `NSR submod`).
- Some R56 Spanish focuses mention states 169 and 173. Effects on states Spain doesn't own simply do nothing, so there are no crashes.
- **Not compatible** with other mods that edit Andalusia's state files.
- The mod adds new files only (a new tag, `NSR_` prefixed ideas and events, and a new faction name). It uses vanilla's generic faction templates, which R56 does not change.

## Troubleshooting

- **The country isn't in the list:** the mod isn't enabled, or it loads *before* R56.
- **You see text like `NSR_exiles_of_sabilla` instead of names:** the localisation file lost its UTF-8 BOM. Don't re-save it with Notepad. Use VS Code or Notepad++ and save as "UTF-8 with BOM".
- **The game crashes on start:** check that you have exactly one copy of the mod, and that the `.mod` file sits next to the folder, not inside it.
- **Error log:** `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Hearts of Iron IV\logs\error.log`. Launch the game with `-debug` to see errors in-game.
